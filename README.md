**The Velvet Vault — A Midnight Heist.** An original top-down stealth game: sneak past patrolling guards and sweeping security cameras, pocket the Star of Midnight and four more treasures, and slip out the service door before the alarm hits 100%. Three nights of escalating patrols, coins to lure guards off their routes, and a limited power cut for the tight spots. Keyboard + mouse, plain HTML/Canvas, no network requests, all sounds synthesized in the browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Or serve `game/` with any static HTTP server and open `game/index.html`.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrows** — move. **Shift** — sneak (slower, harder to spot at the edge of a cone).
- **Hold E** beside loot to pocket it. Grab the **Star of Midnight**, then reach the **SERVICE DOOR** at the bottom.
- **Click** — toss a coin to lure a nearby guard to the spot. **Q** — cut the power for 5 seconds (cameras off, guards frozen).
- Stay out of the yellow vision cones. If a guard or camera spots you, the alarm climbs; at 100% you are busted. A guard that touches you catches you on the spot.
- **R** — retry the night, or open the next night after a clean getaway.
